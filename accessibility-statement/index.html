ACCESSIBILITY STATEMENT

At hairbygerta.com, we are dedicated to making our website accessible to everyone, including those with disabilities. We strive to provide an inclusive online experience for all visitors. If you encounter any accessibility barriers on our site or have suggestions on how we can improve, please contact our accessibility coordinator.

This statement reflects our ongoing commitment to accessibility and was last updated on 11/28/2025.

What web accessibility means to us

Web accessibility means that people with different abilities can perceive, understand, navigate, and interact with a website. My goal is to offer an online experience that is as inclusive as possible for all clients who want to learn about my hair and makeup services in Staten Island, NY.

Accessibility in our organization

We are committed to fostering accessibility not only on our website but also within our physical locations. Our organization has implemented a range of accessibility arrangements to ensure that our facilities and services are inclusive for all individuals.
