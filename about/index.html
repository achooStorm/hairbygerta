About Gerta

I offer hair and makeup services designed to make you look polished and still like yourself. I specialize in modern haircuts, custom color and high-quality extensions that blend with your natural hair. Every visit starts with a short consultation so we can choose the right length, shape and color for your hair and the right makeup look for your event. Before you leave, I show you how to care for your hair at home and recommend products that fit your hair type, so your style stays healthy and lasts longer between visits.
