Keratin and Brazilian Blowout in Staten Island

Hi, I’m Gerta Lila, a hair stylist and makeup artist at Le Vigne Hair Salon in Staten Island, where I’ve worked for about seven years. If frizz fights you every morning, a keratin treatment or a Brazilian blowout can leave your hair smoother, shinier and easier to manage.

What they are

Both are smoothing treatments. A keratin treatment fights frizz for sleek, manageable hair. A Brazilian blowout smooths your hair and gives it a mirror-like shine. They use different products and take a different amount of time.

Who it’s for

For frizzy, puffy or hard-to-manage hair, and for anyone who would like smoother, shinier hair that is easier to style every day.

Your visit

We start by looking at your hair and talking about how you style it now. Then I wash your hair, apply the treatment, blow-dry it and smooth it with a flat iron. A keratin treatment takes about 2 hours 30 minutes, and a Brazilian blowout about 2 hours.

Caring for it at home

  • Before you leave, I’ll tell you when you can wash your hair and tie it back, because that depends on the treatment.
  • Use a sulfate-free shampoo and conditioner.
  • Use a heat protectant before you blow-dry or use a flat iron.
  • Choose a soft scrunchie or a claw clip instead of a tight hair tie.

Questions

Keratin or Brazilian blowout: which one is right for me?

Both fight frizz and add shine. Which one suits you depends on your hair and on what you want from it, so I’ll look at your hair and help you choose.

Will my hair be completely straight?

These treatments are made to smooth frizz and add shine, so your hair is easier to style. How straight it looks depends on your hair, and I’ll tell you what to expect before we start.

How long will it last?

That depends on your hair and on how you care for it, so I can’t promise a number. Gentle products and the tips above help.
