About this Privacy Policy

What type of information do we collect?

We may collect the following types of personal information:

  • Name
  • Phone number
  • Email address
  • Preferred appointment date and time
  • Type of service you are interested in (haircut, color, balayage, highlights, keratin treatment, Brazilian blowout, perm, kids haircut, makeup, etc.)
  • Any notes you choose to send (for example, hair goals, questions, or special requests)

We may also collect some non-identifying information automatically, such as:

  • IP address and general location (city/region level)
  • Device and browser type
  • Pages viewed and time spent on the site
  • How you reached the site (for example, from a search engine or social media)

How do we store, use, share and disclose your information?

This website does not keep a database of visitors. If you book online, your booking is handled by our booking provider (Square), which stores it on its own secure servers.

We use your information only for the purposes listed above. We may share your information with:

  • Service providers that help us operate the website and booking system
  • Email or messaging services we use to send confirmations, reminders or replies
  • Authorities or law enforcement, if we are legally required to do so

These service providers are only allowed to use your information to provide services to us; they are not allowed to use it for their own marketing.

We keep your information only as long as necessary to:

  • Manage your appointments and ongoing relationship
  • Keep required business and tax records
  • Resolve any disputes

After that, we delete or anonymize the information.

We currently do not process online payments through this website. If we add online payments in the future, we will update this Privacy Policy.

How do we communicate with site visitors?

We may contact you using the personal information you provide to:

  • Confirm, update or remind you about appointments
  • Respond to your questions or requests
  • Send important information about changes to our services, hours or policies
  • Send occasional news or special offers, only if you have agreed to receive them

We may contact you by phone, SMS/text message, email, or any method you have provided and agreed to.

How do we use cookies and other tracking tools?

Our website uses cookies and similar technologies to:

  • Help the site function properly
  • Remember certain choices and preferences
  • Understand how visitors use the site so we can improve it

Cookies may be set by:

  • Google Maps, on the Contact page
  • Google Fonts, which provides the website’s fonts
  • Our online booking provider, when you open the booking page

These third-party services may have their own privacy and cookie policies about how they collect and store information.

You can manage cookies through your browser settings. Most browsers allow you to:

  • See which cookies are stored
  • Delete cookies
  • Block cookies or limit certain types of cookies

If you disable cookies, some parts of the site may not work perfectly, but you will still be able to contact us.
